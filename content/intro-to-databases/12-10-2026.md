Natural join - ⋈ - take the Cartesian product of the given relations and then takes the select of matching attributes (i.e. same name and type)

R ⋈ S = σ R.a1 = S.a1 and ... and R.Am=SAm R x S

Semi join - ⋉ - takes the natural join between two tables, with only the common attributes on the right hand side
